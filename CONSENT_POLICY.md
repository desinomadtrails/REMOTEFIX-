## 1. Itemized Consent Framework (DPDP Act 2023)
In accordance with Section 6 of the DPDP Act 2023, consent must be free, specific, informed, unconditional, and unambiguous.

## 2. Consent Collections
- **Registration**: Express consent obtained prior to account creation.
- **Service Request**: Explicit consent to process address and phone details for technician dispatch.
- **Remote Sessions**: Active, explicit user code authorization required for every remote session.

## 3. Withdrawal of Consent
Data principals may withdraw consent at any time by contacting `[INSERT_GRIEVANCE_EMAIL_ADDRESS]`. Upon withdrawal, non-essential data processing is halted immediately.
