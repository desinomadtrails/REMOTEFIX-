## 4. Storage, Security & Retention
- **Databases**: Hosted on Microsoft Azure SQL Database with Transparent Data Encryption (TDE).
- **Files**: Images uploaded during booking creation are validated via magic-byte inspection and stored in secure Azure Blob containers.
- **Retention**: Account data is retained for the lifetime of your account plus 180 days following account closure. Financial records are retained for 7 years as mandated by tax regulations.
